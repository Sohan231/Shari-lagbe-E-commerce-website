Why Shari Lagbe?

Woven in Tangail, sold without the middleman markup

Most sarees change hands four or five times before they reach a shop — each stop adding a margin the weaver never sees. We buy directly from handloom families around Tangail and Pathrail, so more of what you pay goes back to the loom.

Every listing names the weaver's village and the weave technique, because a shari with a story is worth more than one without.
