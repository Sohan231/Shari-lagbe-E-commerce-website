FAQ

Common questions

How long does delivery take?

Most in-stock sarees ship within 2–3 days. Made-to-order Jamdani pieces can take 2–4 weeks since they're woven after you order.

Can I return a saree if it doesn't suit me?

Yes — unworn sarees with tags attached can be returned within 7 days of delivery for a full refund.

Do you ship outside Bangladesh?

Not yet. Right now we deliver nationwide within Bangladesh only, with plans to expand shipping later.

How do I know the saree is genuinely handloom?

Every listing names the weaving village and family. We visit each workshop ourselves before listing their work.

Handloom sarees sourced directly from weavers in Tangail, Bangladesh — delivered nationwide.
